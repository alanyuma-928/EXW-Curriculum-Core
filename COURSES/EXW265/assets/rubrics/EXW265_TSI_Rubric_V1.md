## EXW265: Thermal-Spatial Integration (TSI) Rubric
**Framework:** Mission Loop (Pattern / Rule / Solve)
**SSoT:** PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed), ACSM 12th Ed, Yuma Thermal Safety Protocol

| Criteria | Exemplary (Lead Auditor) | Proficient | Emerging |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mission Loop** | Identifies the "Triple Collision" of condition, meds, and heat. | Identifies conditions and basic heat risks. | Misses environmental interference. |
| **Thermal Shadow** | Maps intensity against facility cooling and hydration. | Notes indoor need but lacks spatial mapping. | Vague "shady" area recommendations. |
| **Metric Integrity** | Enforces RPE 3–5; invalidates HR for beta-blockers. | Suggests RPE but keeps invalid HR targets. | Uses HR-based zones despite contraindications. |
