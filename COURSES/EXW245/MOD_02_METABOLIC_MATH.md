### 🧪 The Simulation: The "Metric-Native" Athlete
**Subject:** 22yo Female Athlete  
**Mass:** ### 65kg (143lbs) ###  
**Protocol:** Walking at $3.5\text{mph (5.6km/h)}$ at a 3% grade.  
**Medications:** None (Clear for Math).

**Student Task:** Using the **Mission Loop**, calculate the $VO_2$ cost. 
* *Hint:* Ensure your final answer is provided in both relative and absolute terms.

---

### 🌟 10/10 Student Exemplar (Snippet)
**Pattern:** Subject is a $65\text{kg}$ female athlete. No clinical redlines detected.
**Rule:** Apply the ACSM walking equation: $VO_2 = (0.1 \times S) + (1.8 \times S \times G) + 3.5$.
**Solve:** $VO_2 = (0.1 \times 93.8) + (1.8 \times 93.8 \times 0.03) + 3.5 = 18.2\text{ ml/kg/min}$.
