### 📖 Core Content: The Evolution of the "GY"
The **AWC GY Building** isn't just concrete and steel; it’s where generations of Matadors have improved their lives. 
* **The Past:** Early gym culture focused on "Sport Performance" for athletes only.
* **The Present:** Following the **PAGA 2018** standards, our gym is a "Health Hub" for students, faculty, and community members of all ages.
* **The "Why" of Kilograms:** We use $kg$ plates because Kinesiology is an international science. Being "Metric-Native" means you can walk into a gym in Yuma, Mexicali, or Tokyo and know exactly how much you are moving.
