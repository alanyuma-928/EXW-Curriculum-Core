### 📖 Core Content (Community Connection)
> **Key Concept:** Have you ever noticed that the big plates in the AWC gym say $20\text{kg}$? Many of our neighbors in San Luis or Mexico grew up using kilograms, while others use pounds. To be a community leader in fitness, you get to be "unit-bilingual." If you know that $20\text{kg}$ is about $44\text{lbs}$, you can help *anyone* in the gym load their bar safely and confidently.
