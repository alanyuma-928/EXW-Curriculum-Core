# EXW150: Nutrition for Sports & Exercise
## 📘 Module 1: Grounding & Source of Truth

### 🛡️ Tier 1 Nutrition Override: The "Food First" Gate
**Ref: _SSOT_ENGINE/0.5-institutional-resources.md**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Clinical Redline:** The "Single Source of Truth" (SSoT) for this course is the **DGA 2025-2030** and **PAGA 2018**. 
> 1. **PATTERN:** Identify if a recommendation relies on commercial supplements over whole-food nutrient density.
> 2. **RULE:** Apply the "Food First" protocol. Supplements are only considered after nutrient-dense whole foods are optimized.
> 3. **SOLVE:** Pivot to a sustainable, whole-food replacement (e.g., Low-fat chocolate milk for recovery).

### 📖 Core Content (SOP Snippet)
> **Key Concept:** Nutritional requirements are often calculated in grams per kilogram of body weight ($g/kg$). For an AWC athlete weighing $80\text{kg (176lbs)}$, a protein target of $1.6\text{g/kg}$ requires an understanding of both unit systems to ensure precise fueling.
