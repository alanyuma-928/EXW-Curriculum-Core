# EXW150 Module 2: Macronutrients & The "Food First" Math
## 📘 Module Overview

In this module, we transition from "What is nutrition?" to "How do we fuel a body?" We prioritize the **Metric-Native** advantage, as all clinical and sports nutrition math ($g/kg$) relies on the kilogram.

---

### 🛡️ Tier 1 Safety Gate: The "Food First" Audit
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Clinical Rule:** Before calculating supplement needs, the student MUST satisfy macronutrient targets using whole foods. Supplements are a "Secondary Solve."

---

### 🎯 Learning Objectives
* **[LO 2.1]** Convert body mass from $lbs$ to $kg$ to calculate daily protein and carbohydrate requirements.
* **[LO 2.2]** Identify the macronutrient distribution ranges (AMDR) per the **DGA 2025-2030**.
* **[LO 2.3]** Use the **Mission Loop** to audit a commercial "Protein Powder" claim against whole-food alternatives (e.g., Greek yogurt or low-fat milk).

### 🌟 10/10 Student Exemplar: The Protein Audit
**Pattern:** I am working with a $75\text{kg (165lbs)}$ athlete. Their current intake is heavily reliant on expensive "Mass Gainer" shakes.
**Rule:** Per the **DGA 2025** and the **Food First** protocol, the goal is $1.4\text{g/kg} = 105\text{g}$ of protein daily.
**Solve:** Instead of the $50 shake, I prescribed a breakfast of 3 eggs and a post-practice $16\text{oz}$ low-fat chocolate milk. This meets the protein timing needs while staying within the "Food First" safety gate.
