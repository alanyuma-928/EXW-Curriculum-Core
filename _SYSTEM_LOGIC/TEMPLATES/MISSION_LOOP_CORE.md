## The Mission Loop (Pattern / Rule / Solve)

### 1. Pattern (The Audit)
* Identify clinical anomalies, environmental redlines, or pharmacological drift.
* Identify Mechanical and Physiological Patterns (e.g., Levers, Torque, and force production).
* Use delimiters (###) to flag radioactive variables.

### 2. Rule (The SSoT)
* Ground the pattern in PAGA 2018 or ACSM 12th Ed.
* Identify the specific clinical redline or safety gate.

### 3. Solve (The Design)
* Pivot to a safe modality (e.g., Nordic Walking, Seated Resistance).
* Prioritize RPE 12–14 and Renal Rest.
