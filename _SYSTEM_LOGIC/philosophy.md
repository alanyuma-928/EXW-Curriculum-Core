# EXW Curriculum Philosophy: The Playbook Model

## <h2> The Architect's Vision
The EXW curriculum is built on the principle of **Institutional Agility**. We do not just teach facts; we engineer problem-solvers. By utilizing a "Curriculum-as-Code" framework, we ensure that every student, from the rookie in EXW101 to the clinician in EXW265, has access to an accessible, mobile-first, and evidence-based education.

## <h2> The SSoT as a Playbook
We treat the **Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans (PAGA 2018)** and the **Dietary Guidelines for Americans (DGA)** as the "Single Source of Truth." In this ecosystem, these are not checklists—they are **Playbooks**. 
- **The Rules:** Federal guidelines that establish safety and efficacy.
- **The Patterns:** Identifying the mechanical (Levers/Torque) and physiological realities of the client.
- **The Solve:** Executing evidence-based interventions that prioritize long-term health over short-term "hacks."

## <h2> The Mission Loop Framework
Every module is a "Mission" designed to reduce the "Perceived Busy Work" and focus on high-fidelity auditing:
1. **Pattern:** What is the mechanical or clinical anomaly? (Use ### Delimiters)
2. **Rule:** What does the PAGA Playbook say about this population?
3. **Solve:** How do we apply "Start Low, Go Slow" to reach the goal?

## <h2> Accessibility First
We adhere to **WCAG 2.1 AA** standards because equitable access is a clinical necessity. All content is designed for the mobile-first community college student, ensuring that cost and technology are never barriers to entry.
