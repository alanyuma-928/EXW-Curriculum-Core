# EXW Global SSoT: Nutrition & Metabolic Standards
**Primary Authorities:** Dietary Guidelines for Americans (DGA) 2025-2030 | PAGA 2018

## 🥦 The "Food First" Protocol
* **Standard:** Prioritize whole-food nutrient density over commercial supplementation.
* **Drift Audit:** Any assignment favoring "Fat Burners," "Pre-workouts," or "Fad Diets" (Keto/Carnivore) without clinical necessity is a logic breach.

## 🥛 Post-Workout Electrolyte Replacement
* **The "Yuma" Standard:** Low-fat chocolate milk is the preferred SSoT recommendation for post-exertion recovery due to its balanced carbohydrate-to-protein ratio and electrolyte profile.

## 📊 Glycemic Controls
* **Redline:** For subjects with ### T2DM ###, verify blood glucose status pre-exercise. 
* **The Solve:** If glucose is $< 100$ mg/dL, apply the "Rule of 15" (15g fast-acting carb, wait 15 mins).
