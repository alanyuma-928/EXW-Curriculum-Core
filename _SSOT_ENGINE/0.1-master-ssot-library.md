# EXW Global SSoT Library: Clinical Redlines
**Primary Authorities:** PAGA 2018 (2nd Ed) | ACSM 12th Ed

## 🛑 Absolute Termination Redlines (Hemodynamic)
* **Systolic BP:** $\geq 250$ mmHg
* **Diastolic BP:** $\geq 115$ mmHg
* **Clinical Breach:** Any recommendation to "push through" these numbers is an automatic audit failure.

## 💊 Pharmacological "Pinned Gauges" (Beta-Blockers)
* **Variables:** ### Atenolol ###, ### Metoprolol ###, ### Propranolol ###
* **The Rule:** These agents chemically suppress HR. %HRR and %MaxHR are **INVALID**.
* **The Solve:** Mandatory shift to **RPE (6–20 Scale)**. Target: 12–14 (Moderate).

## 🌡️ Renal-Thermal Safety Gates (Yuma Protocol)
* **Thermal Ceiling:** Outdoor activity is contra-indicated at $> 104^{\circ}F$.
* **Renal Guard:** For subjects with ### CKD / Stage 3+ ###, a **3:1 Renal Rest Ratio** is mandatory (e.g., 3 mins work, 1 min total rest/hydration).
* **Silent Ischemia:** Heat + Neuropathy masks tissue ischemia. Visual foot checks are required.
